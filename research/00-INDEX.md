## Start here: the 10 most important findings

1. **Workers are ahead of employers, not resisting.** 78% of AI users bring their own tools and 52% hide use (Microsoft/LinkedIn 2024, n=31,000). Twice as many skilled users are blocked by their organisation as ready organisations are held back by people (WTI 2026, n=20,000). Files: 05, 02, 01.
2. **Agent reliability is a technology limit, not only a readiness problem.** CRMArena-Pro success falls from about 58% to 35% when tasks go multi-turn. tau-bench success halves when every one of several tries must succeed. Files: 06, 11, 04.
3. **Measured gains are small so far.** Denmark: about 3% time savings, zero effect on earnings and hours (Humlum and Vestergaard). US TFP growth slowed to 0.8% in 2025. Files: 11, 05, 08.
4. **Breadth is up, depth and profit are flat.** About 3% of US small employers have AI "fully integrated" (Fed SBCS). The UK's depth proxy barely moved since 2023 (ONS). McKinsey's EBIT-impact share is flat at about 37%. Files: 03, 04.
5. **"Share of pilots reaching production" ranges from 5% to 46%** depending on who asks what. "95% of pilots fail" is a misreading of a preliminary, self-selected report. Files: 04, 12.
6. **Human oversight is mostly deference.** Analysts followed an agent's coin-flipped lean 86% of the time (Anthropic RCT, n=50). Users accept 93% of permission prompts. Files: 07.
7. **Context helps only when people write it.** LLM-generated context files reduced coding-agent success; developer-written ones helped slightly (Gloaguen et al. 2026). No framework defines "AI-native" by shared context. Files: 07, 02, 01.
8. **Harm is hitting entry-level hiring before transformation arrives.** GenAI adopters hired about 80% fewer juniors per quarter; junior employment fell about 9% (Hosseini and Lichtinger). No aggregate unemployment effect is detectable. Files: 08.
9. **"Freedom to be more human" is not supported yet.** AI time savings turned into task expansion (Berkeley Haas ethnography); AI collaboration lowered intrinsic motivation (Scientific Reports 2025). Files: 09, 11.
10. **Public opinion is turning against AI, led by the young.** 52% of US adults are more concerned than excited (Pew, June 2026), and under-30s moved most. Unions are winning contract terms, not stopping deployment. Regulation is delayed (EU high-risk duties to Dec 2027 and Aug 2028). Files: 10.
